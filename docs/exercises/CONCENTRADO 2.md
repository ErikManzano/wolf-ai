 Tip: Why warming up with dynamic streching

* Higher body temperature

* Increase respiratory rate

* New flow of oxygen and stored energy to the muscles

* Activation of capillaries in the muscles

* Scar tissue breakup

* Increased aurosal, ethusiasm, eagernes and metal readiness

* Warmer muscles relax easier 

* More ability to lift heavier loads.

Dynamic warm up uses the  your own body weight, for each repetition try to go trought a controlled manner. the main diferrence between dynamic and ballistic streching is the speed of the exercise, during a ballistic strech you try forcibly and rapidly increase the range of motion , with a dynamic strech you gracefully  go trhought the range of motion.

**Foam roller**

Aparte de un buen calentamiento dinamico es buena idea la utilización de foam rollers para adicionalmente aflojar tus musuculos y asistir en la rutura de cicatrices. Si un musculo esta  tieso o contraído, la presión de un foam roller puede permitir al musculo  que se relaje.

Cuando utlizas un foam roller en un grupo muscular tenso. Puedes notar que hay areas donde duele. Esto es un *hot spot,* un grupo muscular tenso. Cicatricez se han formado en esta area y necesitan ser liberadas. En vez de evitar el hot spot. Mantente ahí  en el hot spot por un tiempo hasta que el musculo se relaje.

     **Tips antes de entrenar fuerza**

     1. Often a lifter need to lift a bar or dumbbells off the floor before getting into the starting position, of an excercise(E.g. bent-over row, bicep curl, flat or incline dumbbell bench press or fly, upright row, lying barbell tricep extension, stiff leg, deadlift). To avoid excessive strain on the low back, place the body in the correct position to lift the weight safely and effectively.  
        First, use the correct stance in relation to the bar or dumbbells and properly grasp the bar or dumbbell handles:  
* Squat down behind the bar or between the dumbbells  
* Place the feet between hip- and shoulder- width apart.  
* If lifting a bar , position the bar close to the shins and over the balls of the feet and grasp the bar with closed grip that is shoulder-width(or slightly wider) apart.  
* If lifting dumbbells, stand directly between them and grasp the handles with a closed grip and a neutral arm or hand position.  
* Position the arms outside the knees with the elbows extend.


  Follow these six steps to place the body in the correct preparatory position, before lifting a weight off the floor. The following guidelines also describes how the body should be positioned immediately before the first repetition of a power exercise.


  1.-The bak is flat or slightly arched.

  2.-The trapezius is relaxed and lisghtly stretched, the chest is held up and out, and the shoulder blades are held together.

  3.-The head is in line with the spine or slightly hyperextended.

  4.-The body weight is balanced between the middle and balls of the feet but heels are in contact with the floor.

  5.The shoulders are over or slightly in front of the bar.

  6.-The eyes are focused straight ahead or slightly upward.

  **Breathing Guidelines**


  The best general guideline about proper breathing during a resistance training exercise is to exhale throught the sticking point(the most difficult part of the excersise) of the concentric (exertion) phase and inhale during the  easier part of the exercise (eccentric phase). Typically the sticking point occurs soon after the transition from eccentric phase to concentric phase.


  La major manera de guiarse  acerca de la manera mas apropiada de respirar  durante el entrenamiento de Resistencia es exhalar hasta el punto donde te detienes(la parte mas dificil del ejercicio) de la parte concentric e inhala durante la parte  excentrica. A menudo el punto mas difícil ocurre entre la transición de la parte excéntrica a la cocentrica
